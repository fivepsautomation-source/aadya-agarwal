Climate Tech & Automation

Autonomous Ocean pH Buoy & Chemical Buffer Dispenser

Climate Tech
3M Young Scientist Challenge Project Brief & Circuit Schematics +

Arduino Nano microcontroller interfaced with an analog pH probe and sealed servo dispensing mechanism. When seawater acidity exceeds the predefined threshold (pH < 7.9), the servo releases micro-dosed calcium carbonate slurry to counteract localized ocean acidification.

Buoyancy Balance & Low-Power Telemetry Calibration +

Next steps involve dynamic wave-buoyancy testing in coastal flumes, 5W solar trickle charging with deep-sleep power cycles, and GSM-based IoT telemetry transmitting pH logs directly to a web dashboard.

Environmental Water Remediation & IoT

Biochar AIoT Device: Water Filtration & Adsorption

Water Research
IEEE / ISEC Peer-Reviewed Research Abstract +

Comparative adsorption study evaluating softwood vs. agricultural-waste pyrolyzed biochars. Monitored pH, electrical conductivity, dissolved oxygen, and heavy metal uptake (Pb2+, Cu2+) across multi-stage gravitational filter columns.

Mercer County Science & Engineering Fair (MSEF) Poster +

Bench-scale flow trials measuring flow-rate attenuation and contaminant saturation thresholds over continuous 72-hour filtration cycles, validating accessible low-cost drinking water purification.
