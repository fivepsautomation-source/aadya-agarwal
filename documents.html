Project

Autonomous Ocean pH Buoy

Climate Tech
2025 3M Young Scientist Challenge project brief +

Concept: an Arduino microcontroller reads water pH via submersed probe and triggers a servo to release calcium carbonate from a refillable chamber when acidity passes a set threshold.

2025 3M application notes & next steps +

Next steps: test open-water buoyancy, calibrate dispensing disks, compare mineral buffer options, add solar trickle charging, and explore predictive dosing feedback using Python on edge chips.

Project

Biochar Water Treatment

Water Research
IEEE/ISEC research abstract +

Softwood and agricultural-waste biochars compared for heavy-metal and nutrient removal, using pH, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, and biological response as quantitative measures.

Mercer Science & Engineering Fair poster +

Bench-scale treatment trials compare biochar samples, filtration rates, and track changes in water quality metrics across multi-day exposures.

Project

AI for a Better World

Responsible AI
MIT Solve presentation deck +

Proposed water-quality node: AI-assisted sensing and treatment, 5W solar, two-to-three-day runtime, and GSM/4G telemetry. The governance model strictly enforces human review, fixed safety limits, and transparent decision logs.

Project

MoniVitals: Portable Health Station

Health Tech
Community health clinic pilot specification +

Hardware schematics for non-invasive heart rate, SpO2, and temperature readings under 60 seconds. Bluetooth LE sync to mobile app for tracking baseline vital trends in underserved communities.
