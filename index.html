Aadya Agarwal

Eco-engineer & Equitable Healthpreneur.

The Lawrenceville School · Class of 2029 · Lawrenceville, New Jersey

My vision is a world where clean water and basic health care reach the people it usually forgets — and I want to spend my life building the tools that get them there. I work at the meeting point of responsible AI, hands-on engineering and community science, because the hardest problems in equity are never just technical. Whether it is a biochar filter, a one-minute vitals check or a poem, I am chasing the same question: how do we make care more honest, more equitable and more within reach?
