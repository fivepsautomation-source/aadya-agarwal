Hi, I'm Aadya Agarwal

Eco-engineer & Equitable Healthpreneur

The Lawrenceville School • Class of 2029 • Lawrenceville, New Jersey

My vision is a world where clean water and basic health care reach the people it usually forgets — and I want to spend my life building the tools that get them there. I work at the meeting point of responsible AI, hands-on engineering and community science, because the hardest problems in equity are never just technical. Whether it is a biochar filter, a one-minute vitals check or a poem, I am chasing the same question: how do we make care more honest, more equitable and more within reach?

Project

Autonomous Ocean pH Buoy

Climate Tech
2025 3M Young Scientist Challenge project brief +

Concept: an Arduino reads water pH and triggers a servo to release calcium carbonate when acidity passes a set threshold.

2025 3M application notes +

Next steps: test buoyancy, calibrate the dispenser, compare mineral options, add solar power and explore predictive dosing with Python.

Project

Biochar AIoT Device

Water Research
IEEE/ISEC research abstract +

Softwood and agricultural-waste biochars compared for heavy-metal and nutrient removal, using pH, conductivity, dissolved oxygen and biological response as measures.

Mercer Science and Engineering Fair poster +

Bench-scale treatment trials compare biochar samples and track changes in water quality over time.

Project

AI for a Better World (MIT Solve)

Responsible AI
MIT Solve presentation deck +

Proposed water-quality node: AI-assisted sensing and treatment, 5 W solar, two-to-three-day runtime and GSM/4G telemetry. The plan includes human review, fixed safety limits and decision logs.
