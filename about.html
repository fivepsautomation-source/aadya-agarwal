Personal Narrative

The Story Behind the Science

Aadya Agarwal is a student based in Princeton, New Jersey with roots in India. Her name originates from an ancient Sanskrit word meaning the first — a guiding philosophy she carries into every experiment, prototype, and civic initiative.

Where Disciplines Meet

Drawn to questions that cross traditional boundaries — precision engineering and environmental chemistry alongside creative writing, classical music, and civic advocacy — Aadya is happiest when an insight from one field unlocks something unexpected in another. Whether modeling heavy metal biochar adsorption isotherms or optimizing PID loops for autonomous robotics, she believes that technical rigor is only as powerful as the human conscience behind it.

Life Beyond the Workbench

Her favorite book is Charlie Mackesy’s The Boy, the Mole, the Fox and the Horse, which keeps her anchored in the quiet, transformative power of empathy and persistence. Away from the lab, Aadya is an adventurous spirit and home cook — who loves skiing, horseback riding, farm visits, and once made a 20-foot cliff jump into the crystal-clear waters of a Mexican cenote at age ten.
