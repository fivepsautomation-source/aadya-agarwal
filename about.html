My Story

Aadya Agarwal is a Princeton, New Jersey student with deep roots in India. Her name comes from an ancient Sanskrit word meaning the first — a reminder she carries into every experiment that meaningful change often begins with the willingness to step forward before the path is paved.

A student drawn to questions that cross disciplines — engineering and environmental research alongside creative writing, classical music, and civic advocacy — she is happiest when an idea from one field reveals something unexpected in another. Whether modeling heavy metal biochar adsorption isotherms or optimizing PID loops for autonomous robotics, she believes that technical rigor is only as powerful as the human conscience behind it.

“Nothing beats kindness,” said the horse. “It sits quietly beyond all things.”

— Charlie Mackesy, The Boy, the Mole, the Fox and the Horse

Away from the lab bench and the computer terminal, Aadya is an adventurous explorer and home cook, having experienced whitewater rafting, coastal parasailing, and testing regional culinary recipes for family and friends.
