About Aadya Agarwal

Eco-engineer, student researcher, and builder working where responsible AI, hands-on engineering, and community equity meet.

My Story

Aadya Agarwal is a Princeton, New Jersey student with roots in India. Her name comes from an ancient Sanskrit word meaning the first — a reminder she carries into every experiment that meaningful change often begins with the willingness to step forward before the path is paved.

A student drawn to questions that cross disciplines — engineering and environmental research alongside creative writing, classical music, and civic advocacy — she is happiest when an idea from one field reveals something unexpected in another. Whether modeling heavy metal biochar adsorption isotherms or optimizing PID loops for autonomous robotics, she believes that technical rigor is only as powerful as the human conscience behind it.

Her favorite book is Charlie Mackesy’s The Boy, the Mole, the Fox and the Horse, which keeps her anchored in the power of small, persistent acts of empathy. Away from the lab and the workbench, Aadya is an enthusiastic adventurer and home cook, having gone parasailing, whitewater rafting, and experimenting with regional culinary recipes for friends and family.
