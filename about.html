About Aadya Agarwal

Building at the intersection of autonomous systems, eco-engineering, and human equity.

I am a student at The Lawrenceville School ('29) in New Jersey. My research and engineering work centers on accessible hardware, machine intelligence, and sustainable chemistry that solve tangible community challenges.

From coding autonomous trajectory algorithms using Pedro Pathing for competitive robotics, to formulating low-cost biochar filters and autonomous solar-powered ocean pH buoys, I believe technical rigor is most meaningful when guided by human empathy.
